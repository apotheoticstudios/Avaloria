AVALORIA — POTION RECIPE REFERENCE
==========================================


1. CRAFT RAW POTION
---------------------

Every base recipe produces 6 Raw Tier I potions.

Crafting grid:

  W  C  W
  W     W
  W  T  W

W = Water Bottle
C = Effect Catalyst listed below
T = Tuning Ingredient listed below

Raw potions cannot be drunk or thrown. Complete all desired upgrades and
conversions first, then ferment the potion.


2. POTION TIERS — SMITHING TABLE
--------------------------------

Strong / Tier II:
  Template: Raw Tier I potion
  Base:     Diamond
  Addition: A second identical Raw Tier I potion
  Result:   1 Raw Strong potion (Effect Level II)

Mighty / Tier III:
  Template: Raw Strong potion
  Base:     Netherite Scrap
  Addition: A second identical Raw Strong potion
  Result:   1 Raw Mighty potion (Effect Level III)

Tier duration multipliers:
  Tier I   = 1.00x base duration
  Tier II  = 1.333x base duration
  Tier III = 2.00x base duration


3. SPLASH AND LINGERING CONVERSIONS
-----------------------------------

These conversions only accept Raw, unfermented potions.

  Blast Furnace: Raw Potion        -> Raw Splash Potion
  Smoker:        Raw Splash Potion -> Raw Lingering Potion

Each conversion takes 5 seconds and awards 0.1 experience.


4. FERMENT THE FINISHED POTION
------------------------------

Smithing Table:
  Template: Finished Raw potion
  Base:     Nether Wart
  Addition: Nether Wart
  Result:   1 Fermented potion

Fermentation locks in the potion's type and tier and makes it usable.


5. HOW FERMENTED POTIONS WORK
-----------------------------

Non-instant potions:
  Place a fermented potion in your offhand to receive its effect continuously.
  The potion is not consumed. Its stored duration grows while it remains in the
  offhand. After 5 continuous minutes, its duration is doubled; it continues
  growing at the same rate after that. Removing the potion from the offhand
  resets the growth timer.

Instant potions:
  Healing, Saturation, and Harming are used normally and do not provide a
  continuous offhand effect.

The durations below are the starting durations before offhand growth.

QUICK EXAMPLE
=============

To make a Fermented Mighty Splash Potion of Speed:

  1. Craft two batches of Raw Potions of Speed using Water Bottles,
     Iron Ingots, and Sugar.
  2. Blast the Raw Potions of Speed to turn them into Raw Splash Potions.
  3. Combine pairs of Raw Splash Potions with Diamonds at a smithing table to
     produce Raw Strong Splash Potions.
  4. Combine pairs of Raw Strong Splash Potions with Netherite Scrap to produce
     Raw Mighty Splash Potions.
  5. Ferment each finished potion with two Nether Wart at a smithing table.
